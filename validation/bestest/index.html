    BESTEST (ASHRAE Standard 140)¶

    The BESTEST validation runs in trano, on the models trano generates from the YAML descriptions of the 27 cases of ASHRAE Standard 140 with every library it supports: see trano's BESTEST page for the cases, the acceptance bands and the other libraries. FastSmoothBuildings gates there as Buildings and IDEAS do: pytest -m bestest fails when one of its KPIs leaves its band or drifts from its frozen value. This page is the section of FastSmoothBuildings, as trano rendered it when the library moved to this repository; the analysis of these results, now and along the history of the library, is in the BESTEST report of the analysis.

    The FastSmoothBuildings library gives trano a reduced model of the same building: the closed-loop model whose components rumoca translates to a CasADi ODE for optimal control and calibration. It runs the same YAML files in OpenModelica. Its walls are discretised as Buildings discretises them (number_of_states, at most three sublayers per layer), its zones take the air density of the site and exchange long-wave radiation as the YAML asks (linearised at 20 degC or around the current surface temperatures), its outer surfaces exchange with sky and ground exactly, the gas gaps of its glazings conduct at the temperatures of their panes (long-wave exchange and convection of the gas, as Buildings), its windows are shaded by overhangs and side fins as in Buildings, and its controllers are smooth and without events. It gates as Buildings and IDEAS do, on the 27 cases. The column Buildings gives the frozen value of Buildings for the same case (expected/Buildings.json), so that the gap between the two libraries reads next to the band. How the two libraries compare hour by hour, and how they respond to a change of the parameters, is studied in FastSmoothBuildings against Buildings on BESTEST and a house before and after renovation; how FastSmoothBuildings came to these results is told in the improvement plan.

    105 of 105 KPIs of 27 cases are inside their band. Against Buildings, the loads differ by 1.1 % on average and 4.4 % at most, the temperatures of the free-floating zones by 0.38 K on average and 1.70 K at most.
