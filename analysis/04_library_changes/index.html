    4. What was changed in FastSmoothBuildings, why, and what it did¶

    Every change lives in trano/library/FastModel.mo (now this repository) (and its element templates in trano/elements/library/models/fast_model/ and trano/elements/library/library.json), keeps the model an explicit ODE without events (smooth regStep, smoothMax, smoothMin, smoothLimit, sqrt(x^2 + d^2) for |x|; no when, pre or sample) and was checked to export to CasADi with rumoca 0.10.3 (tests/test_fast_model.py). The effects are measured on BESTEST against the bands and against Buildings (BESTEST, history table) and in the study. The plan they came from, with the gaps still open, is docs/validation/improvement_plan.md.

    # Change Gap it closes, and the evidence Effect
    1 Smoothing of the controllers as parameters (dU, dY, dMax, dT, dtSet, tau), at their previous values a calibration needs to widen the transitions of the thermostats and hystereses (mpctrano FastSmoothBuildings.smooth_controllers) the 27 smoothing parameters of the heat-pump house of the tests (tests/models/house_infiltration_air_water_heat_pump.yaml) are tunable in the CasADi model
    2 Night ventilation (ventilationSchedule) reaches the FastSmoothBuildings zone BESTEST 650, 950 and their free-floating twins ran without their fan: the YAML field was dropped by the template the four cases ventilate as in Buildings
    3 Air density at the site pressure (the weather bus carries the atmospheric pressure) Denver, 1600 m: FastSmoothBuildings used sea-level air for the infiltration and the scheduled ventilation; 940 heating +15.6 %, infiltration responses 1.12 to 1.30 annual heating against Buildings +9.0 % → −0.4 % on average
    4 smoothLimit reaches its bounds exactly (quadratic blends inside [l, u]) it saturated at l + dX and u − dX: the 1 MW ideal systems of BESTEST leaked 1 kW of heating and 1 kW of cooling around the clock (940: 10.1 MWh of heating for a net 1.4) heating and cooling demands right in every ideal-system case
    5 Walls discretised as Buildings (number_of_states of the YAML, at most three sublayers per layer in closed loop, one node per layer boundary for MPC) lumped heavy walls cooled the room faster at night and reheated it slower: 940 night heating 53 against 3 kWh, reheat peak +8 % 940 reheat peak +8 % → +2 %
    6 Single glazing: one balance per pane, no empty array in the record BESTEST 670 did not translate (RGap={} is not valid Modelica for OpenModelica; a branch indexed a gap a single pane does not have) 670 runs and passes
    7 Long-wave exchange in the room around the current surface temperatures when the YAML asks (linearize_emissive_power: false) FastSmoothBuildings linearised at 20 degC whatever the YAML said; sun-lit floors at 40 to 60 degC exchanged too little cooling excess about half a point lower, free-floating peaks up to 0.8 K lower
    8 Exact long-wave exchange of the outer surfaces with sky and ground, sigma*eps*A*(T^4 − TEnv^4) the linearisation at the environment temperature understates the loss of a sun-lit surface by a quarter at 60 degC cooling against Buildings +5.0 % → +2.4 %
    9 Gas gaps of the glazing at the temperatures of their panes (Envelope.Functions.gapConductance: Wright's correlations of the vertical cavity and the long-wave exchange across the gap, as Buildings; the record carries the YAML data, not resistances computed in Python) FastSmoothBuildings held each gap at its EN 673 conductance at 10 degC; a sun-lit glazing at 40 degC conducts a fifth more free-floating temperatures 0.80 K → 0.38 K from Buildings on average (largest 3.52 → 1.70 K); 680FF hourly RMSE 1.17 → 0.53 K
    10 Hydraulic links (sensors, junctions, the water side of the heat pump) at 100 Pa instead of 1000 Pa at their nominal flow measured branch by branch: with the living room valve fully open, FastSmoothBuildings passed 36.6 g/s against 43.9 g/s in Buildings, because the sensor feeding the six radiators took a fifth of the head of a branch; the flow laws were not the cause (trano's Buildings valves and radiators are linearised too) renovated house, set point +1 K: living room +0.75 K (Buildings 0.80, before 0.71), heating response 0.79 → 0.87
    11 Sublayers built in Modelica from the YAML layers, the counts written out by the templates (nSta, nNod, lay, nod, sub) rumoca 0.10.3 sizes arrays and evaluates subscripts only from literal parameters (not from a sum, a function or a record default) the same results within 3e-5, the discretisation in the .mo file rather than in Python
    12 Wall states named alike in both libraries: T the layer boundaries (states of FastSmoothBuildings and FastSmoothBuildingsMPC), TSub the nodes inside the layers (FastSmoothBuildings only) after change 5, T[2] was a boundary in the MPC model and a node inside the first layer in the closed loop; mpctrano copies states by name (closed loop and MPC) mpctrano's flexibility test passes again; the results unchanged (2.4e-5)
    13 Overhangs and side fins (Envelope.Functions.overhangSunlitFraction, sideFinsSunlitFraction, ports of Buildings' Overhang and SideFins) BESTEST 610, 630, 910 and 930 could not be described the four cases pass, within 2.2 % of Buildings (shading); FastSmoothBuildings gates in BESTEST

    The validation tooling was corrected on the way too: the study reads the heating of the ideal systems and the gas and electricity of the productions from their energy meters rather than from samples of their powers (the samples of the morning reheat and of the boiler restarts are caught or missed at random, see the boiler water loop); the flat Buildings model is under-determined, the study renders Buildings with its containers as the BESTEST validation does.

    What was ruled out¶

    • The flow laws of the hydronic circuits (change 10): the gap of the set point response was the resistance of the links, not the linearised valves.
    • The sun on the opaque surfaces (the open gap of open gaps): the irradiance on tilted surfaces, the outside convection with its wind modifier, the long-wave exchange with sky and ground and the discretisation of the walls (tried up to nine sublayers) are those of Buildings; the response to the absorptance stays at 0.91 to 0.93.
    • Energy losses in the water loop of FastSmoothBuildings (the gas of the house before renovation): the loop conserves energy within 0.3 % (the boiler water loop).
